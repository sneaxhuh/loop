# HACKATHON CONTEXT

Agents are becoming increasingly capable at reasoning, planning, and executing tasks, but most agents remain culturally blind.

They understand facts and instructions but often don't understand what people actually like.

Qloo provides a cultural taste graph containing **250M+ entities** spanning areas such as:

- Music
- Film / TV
- Dining
- Fashion
- Travel
- Brands
- Books
- People
- Cultural interests
- Other lifestyle categories

The hackathon asks participants to build:

- an agentic tool,
- an agent-powered application,
- or an integration that gives an existing agent cultural intelligence through Qloo.

The important requirement is:

**CULTURAL INTELLIGENCE MUST BE THE DIFFERENTIATOR.**

The application should outperform an LLM-only approach because Qloo provides cultural information that the LLM itself does not reliably possess.

If removing Qloo leaves essentially the same product, the idea is weak.
